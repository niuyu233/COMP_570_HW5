## Conclusion

Most complaint type is **HEAT/HOT WATER** during the first 2 months and that is because it is cold in these months. It has 81,631 complaints across the five boroughs. The Bronx had the most (30,139), followed by Brooklyn (20,915), Manhattan (17,604), Queens (12,107) and Staten Island (866).

We may assume that the number of complaints is connected to the population of the borough.

## Comparing

In June and July, complaint about hot water reduced significantly (from 81,631 to 6,889). Since it is hot in temperature during the 2 months and necessity of hot water is lower than first 2 months.
